# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that calculates the simple interest earned on a principal amount over a given period of time at a specified annual interest rate.

## Formula

The simple interest is calculated using the following formula:

**Simple Interest = P × R × T**

Where:

* **P** = Principal amount
* **R** = Annual interest rate
* **T** = Time period in years

## Example

If the principal amount is $1,000, the annual interest rate is 5%, and the time period is 2 years:

**Simple Interest = 1000 × 0.05 × 2 = $100**

The total amount after 2 years would be:

**Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest**

**Total Amount = $1,000 + $100 = $1,100**

## Features

* Calculates simple interest
* Calculates the total amount
* Easy to understand and use
* Suitable for basic financial calculations
